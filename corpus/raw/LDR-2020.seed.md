## [Reg 4] General requirements
Every package of pre-packaged food shall carry a label. The information on the label shall be in English or Hindi (Devanagari script), shall be legible, and shall not be false, misleading or deceptive about the nature, quality or origin of the food.

## [Reg 5(1)] Name of food
The label shall declare the name of the food, which shall indicate the true nature of the food and shall be printed in a prominent manner.

## [Reg 5(2)] List of ingredients
The label shall carry a list of ingredients declared in descending order of their composition by weight or volume at the time of manufacture. Where an ingredient is a known allergen, it shall be declared in accordance with the allergen requirement of these Regulations.

## [Reg 5(3)] Nutritional information
The label shall carry nutritional information per 100 g or per 100 ml of the food, covering energy, protein, carbohydrate, total sugars, added sugars, total fat, saturated fat, trans fat and sodium. Where a nutrition claim is made, the amount of the nutrient that is the subject of the claim shall be declared in the nutritional information.

## [Reg 5(4)] Vegetarian or non-vegetarian declaration
Every package shall carry the prescribed mark: a green filled circle inside a green-outlined square for vegetarian food, or a brown filled triangle inside a brown-outlined square for non-vegetarian food. A food containing egg, meat, fish, poultry, gelatine or other animal-derived ingredients other than milk and milk products shall not carry the vegetarian mark.

## [Reg 5(5)] Declaration regarding food additives
Where a food additive is declared in the ingredients list, the label shall give the functional class name followed by the International Numbering System (INS) number or the specific name of the additive.

## [Reg 5(6)] Name and address of the manufacturer
The label shall declare the name and complete address of the manufacturer or packer, or of the marketer where applicable.

## [Reg 5(7)] Net quantity
The label shall declare the net quantity by weight or volume in metric units.

## [Reg 5(8)] Lot or batch identification
The label shall carry a lot number, batch number or code number by which the food can be traced in the manufacturing process.

## [Reg 5(9)] Date marking
The label shall carry date marking in the form of a best before date or use by date or expiry date, as applicable to the food.

## [Reg 5(10)] Country of origin of imported food
Where the food is imported, the label shall declare the country of origin.

## [Reg 5(11)] Allergen declaration
Where the food contains cereals containing gluten, milk, egg, soy, peanuts, tree nuts, sesame, fish or crustacean shellfish, the label shall carry a statement identifying the presence of that allergen.

## [Reg 5(12)] FSSAI logo and licence number
The label shall carry the FSSAI logo and the 14-digit licence or registration number of the food business operator.

## [Reg 7] Packages of small surface area
Where the surface area of the package is less than 100 square centimetres, certain declarations required by these Regulations may be given in a reduced form or omitted, subject to the conditions specified. Whether an exemption applies to a particular declaration is a matter to be determined by the regulatory affairs analyst or the competent authority.
