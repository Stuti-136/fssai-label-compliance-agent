## [Reg 4] General principles for claims
No claim shall be false, misleading or ambiguous. A claim shall be scientifically substantiated and shall not suggest that a food can prevent, treat or cure a disease or disorder.

## [Reg 5] Nutrition claims
A nutrition claim may be made only where the food meets the conditions specified for that claim in Schedule I. Nutrient content claims are assessed on the amount per 100 g for solid foods or per 100 ml for liquid foods. The nutrient subject of the claim must be declared on the label.

## [Reg 6] Health claims and disease risk reduction claims
A health claim, including any claim that a food boosts immunity, supports heart health or helps control a medical condition, and any disease risk reduction claim, shall be made only if it has been approved by the Food Authority and supported by scientific substantiation. Such claims require human review and cannot be assessed from label values alone.

## [Reg 7] Descriptors such as natural, fresh and pure
Words such as natural, fresh, pure or original may be used only where the food is not subjected to processing or additives inconsistent with the descriptor, and the user of the word shall be able to substantiate it. The truth of such a descriptor cannot be determined from label values alone.

## [Schedule I/NRV] Nutrient reference values used for claims
Protein: 60 g
The percentage of NRV in a claim condition is calculated against the nutrient reference value above.

## [Schedule I/Protein] Conditions for protein claims
Source of protein: at least 10% of NRV per 100 g for solid foods; at least 5% of NRV per 100 ml for liquid foods.
High protein: at least 20% of NRV per 100 g for solid foods; at least 10% of NRV per 100 ml for liquid foods.

## [Schedule I/Fibre] Conditions for dietary fibre claims
Source of dietary fibre: at least 3 g of fibre per 100 g for solid foods; at least 1.5 g of fibre per 100 ml for liquid foods.
High dietary fibre: at least 6 g of fibre per 100 g for solid foods; at least 3 g of fibre per 100 ml for liquid foods.

## [Schedule I/Fat] Conditions for fat claims
Low fat: not more than 3 g of fat per 100 g for solid foods; not more than 1.5 g of fat per 100 ml for liquid foods.
Fat free: not more than 0.15 g of fat per 100 g for solid foods; not more than 0.15 g of fat per 100 ml for liquid foods.

## [Schedule I/Sugars] Conditions for sugar claims
Low sugar: not more than 5 g of sugars per 100 g for solid foods; not more than 2.5 g of sugars per 100 ml for liquid foods.
Sugar free: not more than 0.5 g of sugars per 100 g for solid foods; not more than 0.5 g of sugars per 100 ml for liquid foods.
No added sugar: not more than 0 g of added sugars per 100 g for solid foods; not more than 0 g of added sugars per 100 ml for liquid foods.

## [Schedule I/Sodium] Conditions for sodium claims
Low sodium: not more than 120 mg of sodium per 100 g for solid foods; not more than 120 mg of sodium per 100 ml for liquid foods.
